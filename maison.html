A house that
learned to wait.

Élise Vérault opened the bench in 1891 without a sign. The city already knew who repaired a marine chronometer as if it were a letter. The house has kept that tone: if you must be told, you are not yet meant to enter.

We make twelve pieces a year because a thirteenth would require another pair of hands, and the house does not believe in other hands.

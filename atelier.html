One bench.
Forty nights.

Rue des Granges 8

The lamp is not romantic. It is accurate.

There is no line. There is a bench, a brass loupe, and a window that the old town has already decided to keep dim. A movement is begun on a Monday and not spoken of until the case is closed.

The same hands that cut the pinion write the letter. The same hands let Orion fall into the coffret. We do not outsource the last gesture. Gravity is on staff.

Visitors are rare. When they come, they sit. They are not shown a tray. They are shown a lamp, and asked if they can wait.

March 2026

On letting a watch fall

People imagine a cushion. We prefer air. When Orion is finished, the watchmaker does not place it. He opens the hand and lets the piece find the well by itself. If it cannot, the year has been dishonest.

The fall is short. The rotation is once. Anyone who has stood in the atelier will tell you the sound is not a drop. It is a decision.

January 2026

The well in the suede

Each coffret is cut for one diameter. A Nocturne will not sit in an Orion well. We have tried, once, as a joke. The joke did not survive the lamp.

October 2025

Why we still write letters

A list is a crowd. A letter is a room. If you write to the house, you are not entering a funnel. You are asking whether a piece already knows your name.
